How this works

The library

Documents under open licences (public domain, CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA, MIT, Apache, BSD), each with its own licence and credit. Every donation is confirmed by the person who made it, passes automatic checks, and can be taken down on request.

The reader

A small model running on this computer. It answers only from the library, always names its sources, and says so plainly when the library does not have the answer. Sources, not truth.

Trust rules

  • Every document is donated by someone who confirmed it is theirs to share.
  • Every document passes automatic checks (licence, duplicates, spam) before it joins the library.
  • Anyone can report content that should not be here. Reports are checked automatically: clear cases are removed at once, the rest go to a person.
