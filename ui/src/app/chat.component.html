How this works

The library

Documents donated by their owners under an open licence (CC BY-SA). Every donation is confirmed by the person who made it, reviewed by a maintainer, and can be taken down on request.

The reader

A small model running on this computer. It answers only from the library, always names its sources, and says so plainly when the library does not have the answer. Sources, not truth.

Trust rules

  • Every document is donated by someone who confirmed it is theirs to share.
  • A maintainer reviews each donation before it joins the library.
  • Anything can be taken down on request - no questions asked.
