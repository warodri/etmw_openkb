Something here should not be here? Tell us.

OpenKB publishes documents after automatic checks, with no person reading them first. That only works if removing a document is easy and fast. This page is how.

What you can report

  • Your own work, or someone else's, that is copyrighted and was added without the right to share it.
  • Personal or private information about a person.
  • Harmful or illegal content.
  • Wrong or low-quality text.

You do not need an account. On any answer, tap Report this under the sources and it points at the exact document. Use the form below if you saw it somewhere else.

What happens next

  1. You get a report id straight away.
  2. Copyright and personal-information reports hide the document from every answer immediately. It stays hidden until it is decided.
  3. The OpenKB maintainer decides: remove it for good (text and search data deleted), put it back, or leave it as is. Target: within 24 hours.
  4. If you left an email, we tell you the outcome.
  5. Every report and every removal is written to a log that cannot be edited afterwards.

If a source keeps causing valid reports, we stop accepting content from it. Removal does not need a reason or a lawyer's letter. If you are a rights holder, just report it.
