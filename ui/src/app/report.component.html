Something here should not be here? Tell us.

OpenKB publishes documents after automatic checks, with no person reading them first. That only works if reports are easy to make and are checked fast. Reports are checked automatically too, so clear cases are handled in seconds and people only look at the unclear ones.

What you can report

  • Your own work, or someone else's, that is copyrighted and was added without the right to share it.
  • Personal or private information about a person.
  • Harmful or illegal content.
  • Wrong or low-quality text.

You do not need an account. On any answer, tap Report this under the sources and it points at the exact document. Use the form below if you saw it somewhere else.

What happens next

  1. You get a report id straight away, and an automatic check starts.
  2. Copyright. Name who the work belongs to and give an email for them, plus a link to the original. We check your link against the document. If it clearly does not match, the report is dismissed. Otherwise we email the rights holder you named and ask them to confirm. We only remove the document if they confirm. If they do not answer within 7 days we keep it and close the report. While we wait, the document stays up, unless your link is a clear match, in which case it is hidden (not removed) until they answer.
  3. Personal information. Quote the exact text. If it is in the document and it is an identifier such as an email, phone or ID number, the document is removed at once. If we cannot find the text, the report is dismissed. Otherwise a person decides.
  4. Harmful or wrong. The document goes through the automatic checks again. If it fails, it is removed. Several separate reports hide it until a person looks.
  5. Reports that do not hold up are dismissed and logged. A reporter whose reports keep failing is ignored automatically.
  6. A person only handles the unclear cases, with a target of 24 hours (copyright reports wait for the rights holder instead). If you left an email, we tell you the outcome.
  7. Every report and every removal goes into a log that cannot be edited afterwards.

If a source keeps causing valid reports, we stop accepting content from it. Rights holders: if we email you, one click confirms or declines. The form below is for things you saw elsewhere; it goes to a person.
